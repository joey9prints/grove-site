The Grove

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

The Grove ("The Grove," "we," "us") is a private fundraising‑CRM application operated by Cedar Ridge Capital. This policy explains what information The Grove accesses, how it is used and protected, and the choices you have. It applies to the Grove mobile app and the Grove agent service at grove.cedarridge.capital.

The short version: The Grove reads your email only to build your own private relationship record, sends mail only when you tap to send it, encrypts your data per‑account, never sells it, and never uses it for advertising. You can disconnect and delete it at any time.

1. Information we access

Your Google account, with your permission

When you connect Google, you grant The Grove these scopes, and nothing more:

  • Email address & basic profile (openid, email) — to identify which mailbox you connected.
  • Read Gmail (gmail.readonly) — to read the messages between you and your professional contacts so The Grove can build and update your CRM: who your contacts are, which firms they're at, and the history of your conversations. The Grove reads message metadata and body text; it does not modify, delete, or move any mail.
  • Send Gmail (gmail.send) — to send a follow‑up email only when you explicitly review a draft and tap send. The Grove never sends, replies, forwards, or schedules mail on its own.

Information you provide

Notes, contacts, deal stages, offerings, signatures, and settings you enter in the app.

2. How we use it

  • To extract your contacts, accounts, and interaction history from your correspondence and keep them current.
  • To surface follow‑ups, prepare briefs, and draft emails that you review before anything is sent.
  • To operate, secure, and support the service.

We do not use your Gmail data for advertising, we do not sell or rent it, and we do not use it to train generalized AI or machine‑learning models.

3. Service providers (sub‑processors)

The Grove shares data only with the vendors required to run the service, each bound to protect it:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — message text is sent to Anthropic's API solely to extract CRM information (contacts, firms, summaries) and to draft replies you review. Anthropic does not train its models on data submitted through its API. Content is processed to return a result, not retained to build a profile.
  • Google — the source of the Gmail data you authorize, via the Gmail API.
  • Railway — cloud hosting and the encrypted database.

We do not transfer your Gmail data to any other parties except as required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

Google API Services — Limited Use. The Grove's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide and improve the features you request, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train generalized AI/ML models. Human access to your Gmail content is not permitted except with your explicit consent, to comply with law, for security, or in aggregate/anonymized form for operations.

4. How we protect it

  • Encrypted at rest, per account. Each account's data is encrypted with its own key (envelope encryption); your Google refresh token is stored encrypted, never in plain text.
  • Tenant isolation. Database row‑level security fences every query to your account, so one customer's data can never be read by another.
  • Encrypted in transit. All connections use TLS.
  • Least privilege. The Grove requests read and user‑initiated‑send scopes only; it cannot delete or modify your mail.

5. Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active so The Grove can do its job. You can:

  • Disconnect Google at any time in the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking stops all further access immediately.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us (below). We delete it within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your choices & rights

You control the connection and can revoke it whenever you like. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data; contact us to exercise them.

7. Children

The Grove is a business tool not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, in‑app notice.

9. Contact

Questions, or a deletion request: privacy@cedarridge.capital, Cedar Ridge Capital.
